Margin is profit as a percentage of selling price. Markup is profit as a percentage of cost price — the two are different and often confused.

Margin vs. markup — what's the difference?

Margin is your profit as a percentage of the selling price. Markup is your profit as a percentage of the cost price. If something costs Rs 100 and sells for Rs 150, your profit is Rs 50 — that's a 33% margin but a 50% markup.

  • If you're aiming for a specific margin, calculate the required markup separately — they're not interchangeable.
  • Factor in all costs, not just the direct purchase cost — packaging, delivery, and wastage all eat into your real margin.
