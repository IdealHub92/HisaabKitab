Frequently asked questions

Why does my bank give a different rate?

Banks and exchange counters add their own margin on top of the live market rate shown here, so their buy/sell rates will always differ slightly from the mid-market rate.

How often do these rates update?

Rates refresh live from the source each time you load this page, reflecting current market pricing.
