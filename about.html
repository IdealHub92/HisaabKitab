About this site

Hisaab Kitab is a set of free calculators built for everyday financial questions in Pakistan — Zakat, income tax, loan installments, and take-home salary. The goal is simple: give quick, clear numbers without needing to do the math by hand or dig through FBR notifications.

The income tax calculator is kept up to date with the latest FBR salary slabs. The Zakat and EMI calculators use standard, widely accepted calculation methods.

A note on accuracy: Every calculator here gives an estimate based on the information you provide and publicly available rules. It isn't a substitute for advice from a qualified tax consultant, accountant, or religious scholar for Zakat matters. Always verify important financial or religious decisions with a professional.
