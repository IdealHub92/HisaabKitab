Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What this site does with your data

Hisaab Kitab's calculators run entirely in your browser. The numbers you enter — salary, wealth, loan amounts — are never sent to our servers, stored, or shared. Nothing you type into a calculator leaves your device.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand overall visitor traffic (pages viewed, general location, device type). This data is aggregated and anonymized by Google and does not include anything you enter into the calculators.

Cookies and ads

If this site displays ads (via Google AdSense or similar networks), those networks may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your browsing activity. You can control ad personalization through Google's Ads Settings.

Third-party links

Articles or pages on this site may link to external resources (FBR, banks, other references). We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.
