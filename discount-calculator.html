Frequently asked questions

How do I find the original price from a sale price?

Divide the sale price by (1 − discount%). For example, if something sells for Rs 800 after a 20% discount: 800 ÷ 0.8 = Rs 1,000 original price.

How do stacked discounts work (e.g. 20% + 10%)?

Stacked discounts apply sequentially, not additively — a 20% then 10% discount is not the same as 30% off. Apply each discount one at a time to get the correct final price.
