How this is calculated

EMI uses the standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N − 1], where P is principal, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the number of months. Interest is calculated on the outstanding balance each month, so the interest portion shrinks over time while the principal portion grows.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between EMI and flat-rate interest?

Reducing-balance EMI (used here) charges interest only on the remaining balance, so total interest paid is lower than a flat-rate loan charging interest on the full original amount for the entire tenure.

Does this include processing fees or insurance?

No — this calculates the pure installment based on principal, rate, and tenure. Banks often add processing fees, insurance, or other charges separately.

Can I use this for a car or home loan specifically?

Yes, the math is identical — we also have dedicated car loan and home loan calculators with extra context for those specific loan types.
