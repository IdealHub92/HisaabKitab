How this is calculated

Tax is progressive across 8 brackets, from 0% up to Rs 600,000 to 35% above Rs 7,000,000 annually. Each rate only applies to income within that bracket. Read the full slab breakdown with worked examples for the complete table.

Frequently asked questions

Are these slabs for salaried or business individuals?

These slabs apply specifically to salaried individuals. Non-salaried and business income is taxed on a separate, generally higher slab table.

Does this include EOBI or provident fund deductions?

No — this calculates income tax only. Your actual in-hand pay may differ slightly after other employer-specific deductions.

Do I still need to file a tax return if my employer deducts tax?

Yes, salaried individuals are generally still required to file an annual tax return even when tax is correctly withheld at source.
