Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage increase between two numbers?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original value, then multiply by 100. Use the "% increase/decrease" mode above to do this instantly.

What's the difference between percentage and percentage points?

A change from 20% to 25% is a 5 percentage point increase, but a 25% relative increase (5 ÷ 20 × 100). These two ways of describing the same change are often confused in reporting.
