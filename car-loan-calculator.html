How car financing works in Pakistan

Most banks and dealers offer car financing on a reducing-balance basis, meaning interest is calculated on the outstanding principal each month, not the original loan amount. Your monthly installment stays fixed for the full tenure, but the portion going toward interest gradually shrinks while the portion going toward principal grows.

  • A larger down payment reduces both your monthly installment and total interest paid.
  • Compare markup rates across a few banks — even a 1-2% difference adds up over a 5-year tenure.
  • Factor in registration, token tax, and insurance separately — these aren't included in the loan amount.

Frequently asked questions

Does this include processing fees or insurance?

No — this calculates the pure installment based on vehicle price, down payment, rate, and tenure. Banks often add processing fees, tracker device costs, or insurance separately.

Should I choose a shorter or longer tenure?

A shorter tenure means a higher monthly installment but noticeably less total interest paid over the life of the loan. A longer tenure lowers the monthly payment but increases total interest.

Is Islamic car financing calculated the same way?

Islamic financing structures (like Ijarah) are structured differently in principle, but the monthly payment amount is typically comparable to a conventional reducing-balance calculation like this one.
