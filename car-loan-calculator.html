How car financing works in Pakistan

Most banks and dealers offer car financing on a reducing-balance basis, meaning interest is calculated on the outstanding principal each month, not the original loan amount. Your monthly installment stays fixed for the full tenure, but the portion going toward interest gradually shrinks while the portion going toward principal grows.

A larger down payment reduces both your monthly installment and the total interest you'll pay over the loan's life, since you're financing a smaller amount from the start. Most banks require a minimum down payment, commonly 15-30% depending on the vehicle and lender.

  • Compare markup rates across a few banks — even a 1-2% difference adds up significantly over a 5-year tenure.
  • Shorter tenures mean higher monthly installments but noticeably less total interest paid.
  • Factor in registration, token tax, and insurance separately — these aren't included in the loan amount by most lenders.
