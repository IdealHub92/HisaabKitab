Why break-even matters

Your break-even point tells you the minimum you need to sell before a business idea, product launch, or new pricing structure actually makes money. Below this number, you're operating at a loss regardless of how good the product is.

  • Lowering fixed costs or variable costs per unit brings your break-even point down, making the business viable faster.
  • Raising your price does the same, but only if demand holds at the higher price.
  • Use this before committing to a new product line, not after — it's a planning tool, not just a retrospective one.
