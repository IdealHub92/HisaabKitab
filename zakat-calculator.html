How this is calculated

Zakat is due at 2.5% on wealth held for a full lunar year, once your net zakatable wealth (total assets minus short-term debts) exceeds the nisab threshold. Nisab is based on 87.48g of gold or 612.36g of silver — check current rates from a trusted local source before finalizing. Read the full Zakat guide for worked examples and detailed rules.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to pay Zakat on my salary?

Salary itself isn't directly zakatable, but any portion you save and hold for a full lunar year becomes part of your zakatable cash once that year completes.

Is Zakat due on my house or car?

No — personal-use assets like your home, car, and furniture are not zakatable, regardless of their value.

What if my wealth is below nisab?

Zakat isn't obligatory if your net zakatable wealth stays below the nisab threshold for the full lunar year.

Can I pay Zakat in installments?

Many scholars allow paying Zakat in installments throughout the year, as long as the full amount is paid by the time your Zakat year completes.
