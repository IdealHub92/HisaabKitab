How this is calculated

Your monthly gross is annualized, taxed using the current FBR slabs, then divided back to a monthly figure and subtracted from your gross pay. See the full slab table for details on how each bracket is calculated.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my actual payslip different from this number?

This calculates income tax only. Your employer may also deduct EOBI, provident fund contributions, or other benefits that this tool doesn't account for.

Is bonus income taxed the same way?

Bonuses are generally added to your annual income and taxed at your applicable slab rate, though the withholding method employers use can vary.
